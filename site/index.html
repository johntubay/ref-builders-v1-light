How we work

Clear steps. No surprises.

Every project follows the same honest sequence. You always know what is happening now, what comes next and what we need from you.

01
Step 01

See our work

We start by showing you projects we have open, along with photos and videos, so you can judge our construction and finishes for yourself.

02
Step 02

Meet at the office

We meet at our Campbell office to talk through your goals and introduce you to architect and engineering partners if you need plans.

03
Step 03

Design and engineering

We stay involved while your plans are designed and engineered, offering practical input where a detail can be built more simply.

04
Step 04

Agreement after approval

Once your plans are approved, we sign the construction agreement based on the real, approved design.

05
Step 05

Phases, dates and selections

Before each phase you know what is happening, how long it should take and which selections are due, from bathroom valves to kitchen layouts.

06
Step 06

Finishes and turnkey completion

Paula coordinates the finishes and we hand over a completed, move-in ready space, with support for covered warranty items afterward.
